## Day 1 — Warm + close-warm (10–15 touches)

1. Message **everyone who already knows you** (ex-colleagues, clients, friends in tech). Soft ask: “Who needs a landing / UI kit / lite ops desk this month?”
2. Post once on LinkedIn: fixed packages + prices + site link. No long story.
3. Send **5 warm DMs** using template 4 from README.
4. Goal: 2 conversations booked or 1 “send me the brief”.

**Talk track (15-min call):**

1. Confirm pain in 2 minutes (landing? UI mess? ops visibility?).
2. Name the matching package + price + delivery days.
3. Close: Payment Link **or** “I’ll send invoice + kickoff calendar today.”

---

## Day 2 — Cold LinkedIn (20 targeted)

- Filter: Founder / Head of Product / Design Lead · company size 2–50 · location EU.
- Personalize **one line** (product name or recent post) + Landing Sprint or UI System template.
- Cap: 20 connection notes + DMs. Quality > spray.
- Log replies in a simple sheet: Name | Company | Package | Status | Next.

---

## Day 3 — Follow-ups + second channel

- Follow up Day-1/2 non-responders with template 5 (one line, permission to close).
- Email 10 people you have addresses for (same scripts, subject: `Fixed-price landing in 5 days — €790`).
- Optional: post in **one** relevant community (not spammy — value-first + link in comment if rules require).

---

## Day 4 — Offer stacking / urgency

- To warm leads: “I have **one Landing Sprint slot** starting Monday (Europe/Tallinn).”
- Upsell path: Landing → UI System next sprint; Ops Desk for teams already shipping.
- If someone hesitates on €1,490: offer Landing Sprint as wedge, not a discount.

---

## Day 5 — Calls & contracts

- Hold booked calls. Send Payment Link or invoice same day.
- Kickoff checklist: brief (1 page), brand assets, domain/hosting access, success metric (e.g. “form submits” or “screen set approved”).
- If no Stripe yet: bank transfer / Wise + written scope email counts as sold.

---

## Day 6 — Expand EU cold

- 15 more LinkedIn or email touches outside Estonia (DACH, Nordics, NL).
- Reuse winning opener from earlier replies.
- Ask every closed or near-closed lead for **one intro**.

---

## Day 7 — Review & book next week

- Metrics: messages sent, replies, calls, paid, pipeline €.
- Double down on the package that got replies.
- Schedule next week’s 40-touch list.
- Ship or start the first paid job immediately — delivery is the next sales asset.
